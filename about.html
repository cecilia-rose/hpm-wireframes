The math stopped working – so we got started.

Home Properties & More is a Boston real estate company built by people who spent years inside the traditional model and got tired of explaining it to clients.

A renter would find a place, love it, and then learn they owed thousands of dollars to a broker they'd never met. A seller would sign a listing agreement and discover that the person negotiating on the other side had no clear obligation to anyone. Everyone in the transaction was paying for representation, but not everyone was truly being represented.

Section — Our Commitments

A flat fee, and a person who explains things.

We make two big commitments to everyone we serve:

1

Price

You'll know what you're paying before you sign anything, and the number won't move because the deal got bigger.

2

Transparency

We walk you through what happens at each stage, in plain language, before it happens. What an inspection contingency actually obligates you to, why a closing date matters, what you are signing when you sign it. If you've done this ten times, we'll move fast. If it's your first, we'll slow down, and we won't ever make you feel like you have to apologize for asking.

Section — Who We Serve
Image — small Boston landlord / two-family property
~640×480px

Small portfolios. Real people.

Our clients own one condo, a two-family rental, or a six-unit walk-up they inherited and now have to figure out. They're not institutions and they don't have a leasing department.

The national platforms treat them as an afterthought. The traditional brokerages price them like a luxury client. That gap is the entire business.
